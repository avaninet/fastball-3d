Flight guide

Tommi’s tether has snapped. Her suit thrusters are stuck closed. Every tool is one pitch and one chance to change course.

Make the first pitch

Start with Tether separation. Choose Face away, select the spare battery, set power and press Throw. The tool leaves along the crosshair. Your suit recoils in the opposite direction. Turning the visor never steers the coast.

Read the return

The mint drift marker points along your current velocity. The green outline is the full 14 × 14 × 3.2 m grab-bar envelope, including the gaps in the artwork. Read the current coast after release. A shrinking range can still end in a flyby. The mint path and closest-pass marker appear in both cameras when in view. The aft image is unmirrored.

Correct or brake

Use the station marker to reacquire the target. To trim a high pass, throw toward the side you will miss on. To brake, select Face drift and throw a light tool. With soft capture on, enter at or below 0.080 m/s. After a fast entry, clear the envelope and re-enter for another grab.

Flight models and clock

Straight-Line holds your velocity constant between throws. Orbital curves your path in the station’s rotating frame on a 92-minute circular orbit. Both give you 7:30 of simulated oxygen. Aim and read instruments while paused. Fast-forward is available for a committed coast.

Controls

  • Drag the forward view, or tap a point to center it. Arrow keys slew the visor by 2°. Hold Shift for 0.25° steps.
  • Keys 1–6 select equipment. [ and ] adjust power. Enter throws with the visor focused. P pauses.
  • Heading runs from radial-out toward along-track. Positive elevation points above the orbital plane. Your visor keeps the orbital normal up.
