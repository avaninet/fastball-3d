Flight guide

Mission specialist Capt. Tommi Dylan (she/they) broke Mach 10 in the classified SR-73. The former high-school star pitcher still plays with friends on Saturdays. Today the tether parted during a lightweight suit test, and the integrated thruster valves stuck closed. Mission Control would like its drill back.

Pitch from the forward visor

Begin flight starts the oxygen clock. Drag to turn or tap to center a bearing. The tool leaves along the crosshair and your suit recoils the other way. Select a slot, adjust power and press Throw. Turning alone leaves your velocity unchanged.

Read both cameras

STN points toward the station when it leaves the frame. The aft view looks straight behind you. Mint marks show your committed coast and its direction. If the path looks end-on, read the time, side and clearance under Current coast. The whole 14 × 14 × 3.2 m outline is within reach, including the gaps between panels.

Trim and brake

A shrinking range can end in a flyby. Pitch toward the side of a predicted miss to push away from it. Pitch along measured drift to brake. Soft capture requires entry at 0.080 m/s or slower. A fast entry leaves you flying. Clear the volume before trying to grab again.

Time and controls

Straight-Line holds velocity between releases. Orbital bends the coast in a rotating frame. Each flight has 7:30 of oxygen. Pause to read instruments, then resume to aim. Arrow keys turn 2°, or 0.25° with Shift. Keys 1–6 select a tool, [ and ] change power, Enter throws from the visor, and P controls the clock.

Story / guided rescue

First rescue starts a fresh Straight-Line lesson. Story also lets you stage an opening, line up while paused and snap to measured bearings. Throw starts or resumes the clock at 1×. Choose Story flight in setup to use these assists on another opening.
