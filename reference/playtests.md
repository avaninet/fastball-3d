# Open Fastball 3D playtests

These questions need observations from people flying the game. The automated studies cover route feasibility and simulated pilots.

| Exercise | What to observe |
| --- | --- |
| First pitch on Tether separation | Before release, ask a new pilot where the tool and suit will go. After the turn, can they find the station in the aft camera and read their new coast? |
| Brake for a 0.100 m/s approach | Ask what happens under Any-speed, Firm and Soft. Can the pilot find **Face drift**, time a brake in the final 30 seconds, and check the new coast? |
| Trim a miss in Random Dodgeball | Can the pilot use the suggested direction and recoil to choose a light tool and power, then check both debris clearance and the station pass? Try the same Orbital near-frame opening under Firm and Soft in alternating order. Record the first warning noticed, outcome and tools left. |
| Compare a tilted grab profile | Fly near-frame Orbital Soft with `F1B-5001GZ` and `F1B-5011G7` in alternating order. Record when the pilot recognizes the station edge, first predicts contact or a miss, and whether they would choose the tilted flight again. |
| Touch controls on a physical iPhone and iPad | From setup through a flight and debrief, check that taps select the intended control exactly once. Try aiming, changing tools, throwing and opening dialogs in portrait and landscape. Check whether the pilot can see and reach Throw. |

For a future trial of longer Random Drift openings, the [historical study](random-drift-difficulty.md) records the proposed distances and matched-seed method.
