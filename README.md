## The service call

BOREX sent Capt. Tommi Dylan (she/they) outside to cold-reboot an inference server. Yes, it takes all nine tools. Then the tether parts and the thruster valves stick closed. Commander Julian Bern can still see her radar track. Tommi can still put a hand on the wrench pouch.

Tommi is widely rumored to have been the test pilot of the classified “unmanned” X-99 orbital spaceplane. “Let’s just say she’s set records she can never brag about.” &mdash;&nbsp;Commander&nbsp;Julian&nbsp;Bern

The habit that comes back to her now is older than that. She was a high-school star pitcher. She still plays Saturdays with her friends. On a dusty field in the bright sun, this is the moment the crowd goes quiet. Out here she has to pitch her way home. The crew would like their drill back.

## Fly your first rescue

1. Choose **First rescue** for a guided first pitch. To fly on your own, start with **Tether separation** and **Straight-Line**.
2. Face away from the station and throw. The tool goes through the forward crosshair and your suit recoils the other way. Look for the station in the aft camera.
3. Check **Current coast**. **Contact in** means your present path will bring you to the bars. If it says **Miss by**, try a light tool and use **Preview throw** to see where the correction would take you. Open **Guide** in the cockpit for controls, braking and strategy.

## Choose a challenge

**New flight** has six openings. Try **Lateral drift** for a sideways approach, **Wide separation** for a longer return, or **Random Drift** for one of 4,096 curated separations. In **Dodgeball Challenge**, a loose service cover and a bent frame cross your route home. **Random Dodgeball** offers eight layouts with 512 variations each.

Fly **Straight-Line** to coast in a straight line, or choose **Orbital** to see your path bend relative to the station. **Any-speed** lets you grab the bars at any speed. For a gentler arrival, **Firm** requires 0.120 m/s or slower and **Soft** requires 0.080 m/s or slower. **Story flight** gives you reloading tools and time to line up a pitch while paused.

Choose **Manual (no guidance)** to fly any opening by sight. The navigation display goes offline. Use the two cameras, oxygen clock and equipment rack. You control play speed, even during a close approach. Story still lets you line up while paused and reloads each tool. Fly the entire attempt with Manual on to earn **Manual throughout** on your flight card.

## Challenge a friend

Turn on **Random station orientation** in any of the six scenarios to try a different approach. The station holds its new angle for the whole flight. Watch the mint outline in both visors to see how much room you have.

In **New flight**, use **Copy code** or **Copy link** for any of the six openings. For example, paste `F1B-5011G7` into **Challenge code or link** and choose **Load code** to fly a tilted, near-frame Random Dodgeball approach in Orbital with a Soft grab. The code restores Tommi, debris, station angle, flight model, capture rule, inventory and starting aids. Each player flies their own throws.

After the flight, choose **Flight card** in the debrief to save the image or copy the challenge. The upper chart shows radial motion and the lower chart shows cross-track motion. Match the numbered throws to follow the same pass in both views. Compare who made it home, who managed the gentlest grab, and who brought the drill back.

Fastball 3D is a very generous and forgiving approximation of a space emergency scenario that has fortunately never occurred. **Do not try this on orbit.**
